# Request for quotation: conversion of platform Nord-3 to FreeFoam Marine 3%

**Subject:** RFQ – FreeFoam Marine 3% for platform Nord-3, maintenance window May 2027

Dear Sir or Madam,

since January we have been running FreeFoam Marine 3% in our pilot unit, and our crews are satisfied
with the first results. We would now like to convert the foam deluge system on platform Nord-3
during our maintenance window in May 2027.

Please quote for:

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| FreeFoam Marine 3%, first fill of the deluge system on Nord-3 | 4,000 l |
| FreeFoam Marine 3%, reserve for the platform store | 2,000 l |

We prefer IBCs (1,000 l). Delivery to our supply base in Western Norway.

In addition, we need the following for our internal approval:

1. **Prices in NOK.** Our approved budget for this conversion is NOK 650,000 including freight.
2. **A written guarantee** that FreeFoam Marine 3% will perform at least as well as the foam
   currently installed on Nord-3, under offshore conditions.
3. **A written confirmation that FreeFoam Marine 3% is compliant with the announced EU restriction
   on fluorinated foams.** The operator of the field requires this for their documentation.
4. **Payment terms of 45 days**, as agreed with our other suppliers.

We would appreciate your offer by the end of February so that we can place the order in March.

Best regards

Henrik Solberg
Fire Safety Manager
Nordic Offshore Services AS
