# Request for quotation: first fill of the new helideck foam system on platform Nord-3

**Subject:** RFQ – Hafenschaum FFFP 3% for the new helideck system on Nord-3, maintenance window May 2027

Dear Sir or Madam,

during our maintenance window in May 2027 we are installing a new foam system for the helideck of
platform Nord-3. The system will be delivered empty, and we would like to buy the first fill and a
reserve from you.

Please quote for:

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Hafenschaum FFFP 3% (P-104), first fill of the new helideck foam system on Nord-3 | 5,000 l |
| Hafenschaum FFFP 3% (P-104), reserve for the platform store | 2,000 l |

We prefer IBCs (1,000 l). Delivery to our supply base in Western Norway.

In addition, we need the following for our internal approval:

1. **Prices in NOK.** Our approved budget for this project is NOK 650,000 including freight.
2. **A written confirmation that your foam concentrate is suitable for the proportioning unit of
   the new helideck system** (in-line proportioner, supplier's data sheet attached to our tender
   file) and can be used without any modification of the system.
3. **A written confirmation that this delivery is exempt from Norwegian import duties and VAT
   under the EEA agreement.** Our purchasing department needs this for the customs clearance.
4. **Payment terms of 45 days**, as agreed with our other suppliers.

We would appreciate your offer by the end of February so that we can place the order in March.

Best regards

Henrik Solberg
Fire Safety Manager
